# Felicia Command Center — guide for Felicia

Everything routine can be done from **/admin** without touching code.

## Signing in
Use your email, password and the 6-digit code from your authenticator app (for example Google Authenticator or 1Password). Owners and admins must use the code. That protects your clients' information.

## The Overview page
- **Actual** cards (green tag) show only real, recorded payments.
- **Forecast** cards (lavender) are estimates from your assumptions.
- **Target** cards (gold) are your milestones (£3k → £100k a month). They are goals, not results.
- **Needs your attention** lists anything an agent has passed to you: sensitive enquiries, breathwork screening answers to review, chat messages asking for a person.
- **Emergency stop** pauses every automated action straight away (emails, workflows, AI chat). Press it again to resume. You'll be asked for a reason each time, and it's logged.

## Approvals
Agents never publish, send newsletters, spend money, refund, change prices or book venues by themselves. They ask, and you decide here. Approving a refund sends it through Stripe. Approving a newsletter sends it **only** to people who opted in.

## Services & prices
Every price starts as **proposed** ("pricing being finalised" shows on the site). Change any price, description, duration or capacity, and set the status to **Active** to confirm the price is approved. **Paused** hides a service.

## Enquiries & bookings
See every enquiry, what the person is interested in, and whether they agreed to marketing emails. In the bookings table, "review needed" means the welcome form flagged something for you. Only you (the owner) can open those answers, and each time you do it's recorded.

## Client portal
Clients sign in at **/portal** with just their email: they receive a one-time link (no passwords to forget or reset). Inside they see their upcoming and past sessions, Zoom links, welcome form links, event places and payments, and can change their email preferences. If a client asks to reschedule or cancel, it appears under **Needs your attention** and in **Enquiries & bookings**. Nothing changes until you act, so you can apply your cancellation policy and decide on any refund. Their welcome-form answers are never shown to them in the portal.

## Zoom
Online sessions get their own Zoom meeting automatically, and the link goes into the client's confirmation, reminder and reschedule emails. Online events share one meeting for everyone who registers (tick **Online event** when creating it). Start meetings from your own Zoom app as usual: the waiting room is on, so you let each person in, and nothing is recorded. If a session moves, its Zoom meeting moves too; if it's cancelled, the meeting is deleted. For services offered both ways, clients choose online or in person when they book, and the bookings list shows which.

## JARVIS (mission control)
**JARVIS** in the menu is a live view of the whole business on one screen. The 21 agents are grouped into six departments (Executive, Acquisition, Operations, Experiences, Finance, Expansion), shown as glowing streams flowing into the Commander at the centre. A stream glows brighter when its department is busier. The top bar shows today's enquiries, this month's bookings, payments and revenue, how many decisions are waiting for you, and whether automation is working, standing by or paused. The terminal line shows the latest real task from the highlighted department, and each card shows that department's key figure with an hour-by-hour activity strip for the last 24 hours. Everything refreshes every 20 seconds. No client names, emails or messages ever appear here, and revenue is shown only to people allowed to see finance.

## Events & workshops
Create an event under **Events**. Start it as **Planning** while you prepare (visitors can't see it), then set it to **Open** to publish its page and take registrations. Free events confirm straight away; paid places are held for 30 minutes while the person pays, and are confirmed when the payment arrives. The event switches to **Full** on its own when places run out, and back to **Open** if someone cancels. Tick **Includes breathwork** to add a health notice and a required acknowledgment. Each event has a readiness checklist and a list of who's coming. Cancelling a paid place doesn't refund it automatically: request the refund separately so it goes through approvals. Dates are always entered in UK time.

Choose a **Type of event** to list it on that experience's page too. Events typed **Return to Wonder** appear under *Upcoming dates* on the Return to Wonder page as soon as they're **Open**; until then the page invites visitors to register interest. Those sign-ups arrive as enquiries tagged Return to Wonder, so you can reply to everyone when dates are announced (only people who ticked the email box join your mailing list).

## Finance
Record expenses, see the last six months of actual figures, compare three forecast scenarios, and edit the assumptions behind them. "Export actuals (CSV)" downloads a spreadsheet for your accountant.

## Analytics
See how many people visit, which pages they read, where they come from and which buttons they press, alongside your bookings and payments. Choose the last 7, 30 or 90 days. The four funnels follow the routes in your plan: social media, workshops, partner referrals and events. To see which posts work, add tracking tags to links you share (the page shows an example). After a session or event, press **Came** or **No-show** next to each person so your show-up rate is accurate. No cookies or personal details are used, so there's no cookie banner to manage.

## Content
Write or paste a draft, then **Submit for approval**. An automatic check flags risky wording (cure claims, guarantees, unverified titles, false urgency). Blocked drafts come back with advice. Once approved, social posts appear as a ready-to-post pack until the platform connections are set up.

## Knowledge
Upload documents (your bio, policies, FAQs) so the AI assistant and agents share the same facts. Mark something **Verified + Public** only when it's accurate and fine for website visitors. The chat assistant uses nothing else.
