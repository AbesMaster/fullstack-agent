# Felicia Command Center — guide for Felicia

Everything routine can be done from **/admin** without touching code.

## Signing in
Use your email, password and the 6-digit code from your authenticator app (for example Google Authenticator or 1Password). Owners and admins must use the code. That protects your clients' information.

## The Overview page
- **Actual** cards (green tag) show only real, recorded payments.
- **Forecast** cards (lavender) are estimates from your assumptions.
- **Target** cards (gold) are your milestones (£3k → £100k a month). They are goals, not results.
- **Needs your attention** lists anything an agent has passed to you: sensitive enquiries, breathwork screening answers to review, chat messages asking for a person.
- **Emergency stop** pauses every automated action straight away (emails, workflows, AI chat). Press it again to resume. You'll be asked for a reason each time, and it's logged.

## Approvals
Agents never publish, send newsletters, spend money, refund, change prices or book venues by themselves. They ask, and you decide here. Approving a refund sends it through Stripe. Approving a newsletter sends it **only** to people who opted in.

## Services & prices
Every price starts as **proposed** ("pricing being finalised" shows on the site). Change any price, description, duration or capacity, and set the status to **Active** to confirm the price is approved. **Paused** hides a service.

## Enquiries & bookings
See every enquiry, what the person is interested in, and whether they agreed to marketing emails. In the bookings table, "review needed" means the welcome form flagged something for you. Only you (the owner) can open those answers, and each time you do it's recorded.

## Finance
Record expenses, see the last six months of actual figures, compare three forecast scenarios, and edit the assumptions behind them. "Export actuals (CSV)" downloads a spreadsheet for your accountant.

## Content
Write or paste a draft, then **Submit for approval**. An automatic check flags risky wording (cure claims, guarantees, unverified titles, false urgency). Blocked drafts come back with advice. Once approved, social posts appear as a ready-to-post pack until the platform connections are set up.

## Knowledge
Upload documents (your bio, policies, FAQs) so the AI assistant and agents share the same facts. Mark something **Verified + Public** only when it's accurate and fine for website visitors. The chat assistant uses nothing else.
