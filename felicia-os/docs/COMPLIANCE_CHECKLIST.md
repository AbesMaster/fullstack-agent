# Compliance checklist (for professional review)

This is research and risk-flagging, **not legal advice**. Each item needs confirmation by a qualified professional (solicitor, accountant, insurer). "Built-in" means the software supports the requirement. The business process still needs to be confirmed.

## UK GDPR / Data Protection Act 2018
- [ ] ICO data protection fee paid; controller details in the privacy policy
- [ ] Lawful bases confirmed (contract for bookings; explicit consent for health screening; consent for marketing)
- [ ] Special category data: Article 9 condition (explicit consent) and an appropriate policy document
- [ ] DPIA for processing health data and using AI tools (recommended)
- [ ] Data processing agreements with Stripe, HubSpot, Google, Zoom, Resend, Anthropic and the host; international transfer safeguards
- [ ] Retention schedule decided; deletion process for subject requests
- Built-in: data minimisation in CRM, encrypted intake, owner-only access, audit trail of intake reads

## PECR (marketing email)
- [ ] Marketing only with opt-in consent (built-in: unticked boxes, consent timestamp and source stored)
- [ ] Unsubscribe in every marketing email (built-in: link + RFC 8058 one-click)
- [ ] Cookie use reviewed. The site sets only the admin session cookie (strictly necessary). Built-in analytics store nothing on the visitor's device, keep no IP addresses or personal data, honour Global Privacy Control, and rotate the visitor salt daily. Confirm with the solicitor that no consent banner is needed, and mention the analytics in the privacy policy

## Advertising (ASA / CAP Code)
- [ ] No claims to treat or cure medical or psychological conditions (built-in claim checker blocks common forms)
- [ ] No guaranteed outcomes; testimonials genuine, consented and not implying cures
- [ ] Protected or professional titles only once verified
- [ ] Any "limited places" statements literally true

## Consumer law
- [ ] Consumer Contracts Regulations: pre-contract information, 14-day cancellation right and the express request to start early
- [ ] Consumer Rights Act: services performed with reasonable care and skill
- [ ] DMCC Act 2024: no fake reviews, no drip pricing; clear total prices
- [ ] Cancellation and refund policy final

## Health and safety (breathwork)
- [ ] Screening questions and contraindication policy reviewed with the insurer or a qualified adviser
- [ ] Emergency procedure for in-person sessions; first-aid cover for retreats
- [ ] Venue risk assessments for Return to Wonder outdoor activities

## Business, tax and payments
- [ ] Business structure (sole trader / limited company) and HMRC registration
- [ ] VAT threshold monitoring (UK threshold £90,000 rolling 12 months as of 2024; confirm current figure)
- [ ] Stripe/PayPal terms reviewed (wellness businesses sometimes need extra disclosures)
- [ ] Bookkeeping integration (e.g. Xero) and accountant sign-off on revenue recognition

## Qatar expansion (research only; nothing launched)
- [ ] Business presence route (Qatar Financial Centre vs Ministry of Commerce and Industry licence) and activity classification for wellness services
- [ ] Whether wellness/breathwork services need Ministry of Public Health licensing
- [ ] Advertising and content norms; data protection (Qatar PDPPL, Law No. 13 of 2016)
- [ ] Tax: UK residence and the statutory residence test on relocation, Qatar corporate tax on non-Qatari-owned profits, and any UK–Qatar double taxation treaty effects. **Relocation does not automatically remove UK tax liability**
- [ ] Professional adviser questions prepared before any commitment
